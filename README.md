## 🎯 Objective
Analyze marketing funnel data to identify 
conversion drop-offs, channel performance, 
and opportunities to improve lead-to-customer 
conversion rate.

---

##  Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Total Contacts | 45,211 |
| Converted Customers | 5,289 |
| Conversion Rate | 11.70% |
| Avg Call Duration | 258 seconds |

---

##  Key Insights

###  Contact Method
- Cellular = highest conversion channel
- Telephone = lowest conversion rate
- Unknown contact = very low conversion

###  Monthly Performance
- May = highest contact volume
- March, September, October = 
  best conversion rates
- December = lowest activity month

###  Job Segment
- Management = highest conversions
- Blue-collar = most contacted
- Students & Retired = highest 
  conversion rate percentage

###  Education Level
- Secondary education = most contacts
- Tertiary education = better 
  conversion rate
- Unknown education = lowest conversion

###  Call Duration Impact
- Longer calls = higher conversion
- Avg 258 seconds for all contacts
- Converted customers had longer calls

---

##  Funnel Analysis
Total Contacts:  45,211  (100%)
↓
Engaged Leads:   ~30,000  (66%)
↓
Interested:      ~10,000  (22%)
↓
Converted:        5,289   (11.7%)
---

##  Recommendations

1. **Focus on cellular channel**
   - Highest conversion rate
   - Prioritize mobile outreach

2. **Target management & tertiary educated**
   - Higher conversion probability
   - Better ROI on marketing spend

3. **Increase call duration**
   - Longer engagement = more conversions
   - Train agents for better conversations

4. **Focus campaigns in March & September**
   - Best conversion months
   - Avoid heavy spending in December

5. **Re-engage unknown contacts**
   - Large untapped segment
   - Identify and classify them
